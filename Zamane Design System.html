Zamane

زمان

The design language of a private space for two. Goals, trips and shopping, shared by a couple and drawn like a home in Morocco: sand walls, zellige blue, saffron light, arched doorways.

Warm and calm. Never a bank, never a toy. Everything below is the system the app is built from.

Principles

Five rules every screen follows.

  • Ours, not mineSpeak to the couple as "you two". Show both partners side by side, with equal weight.
  • Who did what, alwaysEvery contribution, note and item carries the partner's avatar and first name.
  • Ideas look lighter than commitmentsA trip Place has a dashed outline. A plan entry is a solid card. Never style the two alike.
  • Numbers are earned, not typedTotals come from history, so the history is one tap away. Figures use tabular numerals.
  • Quick captureEvery add form opens with the name field focused. Everything else is optional and folded below.

Components

Live React components from window.Zamane. They follow the theme toggle.

Button

Verb-first actions. Primary (majorelle) once per view, for the thing the screen is for.

  • primary the one main action
  • secondary default, outlined
  • ghost low-weight actions inside cards
  • danger destructive, always confirmed
  • size="sm" in rows, block for sheets

PartnerAvatar

Who did it. The initial on a tinted disc, ringed in the partner's colour.

  • partner="a" created the space: majorelle
  • partner="b" joined with the code: terracotta
  • Always set the first name beside a small avatar
  • AvatarPair for the couple in headers and Profile

Progress

Goal and budget bars. Turns zellige with "Done" at 100%, danger with "Over by…" past a budget.

  • value always computed from contributions or plan entries
  • mode="budget" for trip spending
  • Animate only when a new contribution lands

GoalCard

A shared goal in a list or on Home: arch cover, amount or percent, progress, who touched it last.

  • kind money (saved / target) or general (percent)
  • No photo: the title's initial in the arch
  • On Home, closest to done first, three at most

TripCountdown

The next or current trip. Status comes from the calendar, never set by hand.

  • Before start: N days to go
  • During: Day X of N, "Happening now"
  • After: N days ago, block falls back to sunken

ShoppingItem

One row of the shared list. Tap a checkbox to try it.

  • Bought: struck through, zellige check, "Bought by…"
  • Rows sit flush under a category header with a subtotal
  • At least 48px tall for touch

Voice

Sentence case, verbs first, plural "you", first names.

Write

  • Add contribution
  • Sara added 500 · bonus month
  • 14,250 MAD of 20,000 MAD
  • in 12 days · Day 2 of 5
  • No trips yet. Plan your first one.
  • Goal reached.

Avoid

  • Submit
  • User 1 updated the record
  • MAD 14250.00
  • Trip starts: 2026-11-21
  • Nothing here yet :(
  • Woohoo!! You did it!!!

Money uses one currency per space, written after the amount, no decimals unless typed. Dates read "Fri 14 Mar", times 24-hour "09:00". People may use emoji in their own notes; the interface never does.
